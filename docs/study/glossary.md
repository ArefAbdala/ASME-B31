# Glossary

Short definitions in plain language. The paragraph after each term is where to read the Code's own definition or rule in the free PDFs. Definitions differ slightly between sections; the section's own definition always governs.

Addenda
:   Replacement pages issued between editions. Each addendum starts with a summary of changes. B31.1-2007 has addenda a (2008) and b (2009); B31.5-2001 has a (2004).

Allowable stress (S)
:   The stress a material may be designed to at a given temperature, from the section's stress tables. B31.1 para. 102.3 and Appendix A; B31.9 para. 902.3 and Appendix I.

Boiler external piping (BEP)
:   Piping from a power boiler to the first stop valve (or valves) of its connected systems. Designed to B31.1 under ASME Section I jurisdiction. B31.1 Fig. 100.1.2, para. 122.1.

Category D, Category M
:   B31.3 fluid service categories: D for nonflammable, nontoxic fluids at low pressure and temperature; M for toxic fluids where a small exposure can cause serious harm. B31.3 only (no free PDF).

Cathodic protection
:   Electrical protection of buried or submerged steel against corrosion, using sacrificial anodes or impressed current. B31.4 Chapter VIII; B31.8 Chapter VI and Appendix K.

Covered piping systems (CPS)
:   High-energy power piping that B31.1-2007 Chapter VII asks owners to track with O&M procedures, condition assessments and records.

Design factor (F)
:   In B31.8, the fraction of SMYS a pipe may be designed to, set by location class (0.80 to 0.40). B31.8 Table 841.114A.

Design pressure
:   The pressure the piping is designed for: the most severe coincident pressure and temperature expected. B31.1 para. 101.2; B31.4 para. 401.2.2; B31.5 para. 501.2.

Direct assessment
:   An integrity assessment that combines records, above-ground surveys and excavation to find corrosion, used where in-line inspection is not possible. B31.8S Appendix B.

Displacement stress range
:   The stress range caused by thermal expansion and other imposed displacements, checked in a flexibility analysis. B31.1 para. 119 and 102.3.2.

High consequence area (HCA)
:   In US gas pipeline regulation, an area where a failure would have the greatest consequences, determined with the potential impact radius. B31.8S section 3 and 49 CFR 192 Subpart O.

Hoop stress
:   The circumferential stress in a pipe wall from internal pressure, approximately \(PD/2t\). Pipeline sections limit it as a percentage of SMYS.

Hydrostatic test
:   A pressure test with water, the default strength test in every section. B31.1 para. 137.4; B31.4 para. 437.4; B31.8 para. 841.3; B31.9 para. 937.3.

In-line inspection (ILI)
:   Inspection with a tool ("pig") that travels inside the pipeline and measures metal loss, dents or cracks. B31.8S section 6.

Joint factor (E)
:   A factor for the longitudinal weld seam of the pipe (1.0 for seamless and most modern welded pipe). B31.4 Table 402.4.3; B31.8 Table 841.115A.

Location class
:   B31.8's measure of population density along a gas pipeline, from Class 1 (rural) to Class 4 (multistory buildings). B31.8 para. 840.2.

MAOP
:   Maximum allowable operating pressure: the highest pressure at which a pipeline may be operated under the Code. B31.8 para. 805.214; B31.4 uses the equivalent for liquids.

Mill tolerance
:   The amount by which a pipe's actual wall may be thinner than nominal under its purchase specification, often 12.5% for seamless pipe. Added on top of the calculated wall thickness.

Owner
:   The party responsible for choosing the Code section and ensuring compliance. See the Introduction of each section.

Pneumatic test
:   A pressure test with air or gas. Used where water cannot be, with extra precautions, because stored energy in compressed gas is much higher. B31.1 para. 137.5; B31.5 para. 538.4; B31.9 para. 937.4.

Postweld heat treatment (PWHT)
:   Heating a weld after welding to temper it and relieve residual stress, required by material and thickness. B31.1 para. 132 and Table 132.

Potential impact radius
:   B31.8S's estimate of the distance a gas pipeline rupture could affect: \(r = 0.69\,d\sqrt{p}\) for natural gas. B31.8S section 3.

Secondary coolant
:   A liquid such as brine or glycol used to carry heat in a refrigeration system without changing state. B31.5 para. 500.2.

SMYS
:   Specified minimum yield strength of the pipe, from its purchase specification (for example 52,000 psi for API 5L X52). The basis of pipeline design.

Stress intensification factor (SIF, i)
:   A multiplier that accounts for higher stresses in fittings such as elbows and tees in a flexibility analysis. B31.1 Appendix D; B31.8 Appendix E.

Temperature derating factor (T)
:   In B31.8, a factor that lowers design pressure above 250°F. B31.8 Table 841.116A.

Weld joint efficiency
:   A factor that reduces the allowable stress of welded pipe according to its seam type and examination. B31.1 para. 102.4.3 (longitudinal weld joint efficiency factors).
