# B31.9 Building Services Piping

**Free PDF:** ASME B31.9-2008 ([open PDF](https://archive.org/download/gov.law.asme.b31.9.2008/asme.b31.9.2008.pdf) · [archive.org page](https://archive.org/details/gov.law.asme.b31.9.2008)). Current edition: 2025 ([ASME](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b31-9-building-services-piping)).

## At a glance

B31.9 covers piping in **industrial, institutional, commercial and public buildings, and multi-unit residences**, inside the building or within its property limits, that does not need the range of sizes, pressures and temperatures covered by [B31.1](b31-1.md) (para. 900.1.1). It is the simplest of the construction sections and a good first book to read in full.

## The limits (para. 900.1.2, 2008 edition)

Piping must stay inside **all** of these limits; above any of them, use B31.1.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Steam and condensate | 150 psig (1 034 kPa) and 366°F (186°C) |
| Liquids | 350 psig (2 413 kPa) |
| Nonflammable liquids, temperature | 250°F (121°C) |
| Compressed air and gas | 150 psig (1 034 kPa) |
| Other gases and vapors, temperature | 200°F (93°C) |
| Vacuum | 1 atm external pressure |
| Minimum temperature, all services | 0°F (−18°C) |
| Carbon steel | NPS 48 and 0.50 in. wall |
| Stainless steel | NPS 24 and 0.50 in. wall |
| Aluminum; brass and copper | NPS 12 (copper tubing to 12.125 in. OD) |
| Thermoplastics; reinforced thermosetting resin | NPS 24; 24 in. nominal |
| Ductile iron | NPS 48 |

B31.9 does not apply to economizers, heaters, pumps, tanks, heat exchangers or equipment covered by the Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code (900.1.3). Boiler external piping follows the jurisdiction drawn in Fig. 900.1.2.

## Key ideas

**It is about limits.** Most B31.9 questions are really "is this inside B31.9 or does it belong in B31.1?" Learn the table above.

**Chilled water belongs here, refrigerant does not.** B31.9 defines chilled water as water or antifreeze used as a brine below 60°F (16°C). Refrigerant piping is [B31.5](b31-5.md).

**Leak testing.** Para. 937.3.4 sets the hydrostatic test at **at least 1.5 × design pressure** at every point, not above the rating of any component, held at least 10 minutes before examining for leaks. A pneumatic test may not exceed **1.25 × design pressure** (937.4.4), is raised in stages, and is held at least 10 minutes before examination.

**Support spans.** Figs. 921.1.3-1 and -2 give suggested maximum spans between supports for steel, copper and thermoplastic pipe, one of the most-used pages in the book.

## Self-check

??? question "Low-pressure steam at 15 psig to a building's radiators: B31.9 or B31.1?"
    B31.9: steam and condensate up to 150 psig and 366°F are in its scope.

??? question "A hospital's 200 psig steam main from its own boiler house: B31.9 or B31.1?"
    B31.1, because 200 psig is above B31.9's 150 psig limit for steam.

??? question "What is the minimum hydrostatic test pressure in B31.9-2008?"
    1.5 × design pressure at every point, held at least 10 minutes (para. 937.3.4).

??? question "Does B31.9 cover a hot water loop at 280°F?"
    No. Nonflammable liquids are limited to 250°F, so a 280°F loop belongs in B31.1.
