# The PDF library: which PDF covers which scope

Ten free PDFs cover six of the seven B31 construction scopes. They are older editions (1991 to 2009) that US regulations incorporate by reference, published by Public.Resource.Org. Only process plants (B31.3) and hydrogen (B31.12) have no free PDF; see [Scopes no free PDF covers](#scopes-no-free-pdf-covers).

These editions are good for learning the Code and finding the right section. For design or compliance work, use the edition your regulator or contract names, usually a newer one (see [sources](sources.md)).

| Scope | Open this PDF | Edition | Covers | Start at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power and central heating | [ASME B31.1 Power Piping](https://archive.org/details/gov.law.asme.b31.1.2007) | 2007 | Power stations, industrial and institutional plants, geothermal and district heating, boiler external piping | para. 100.1 |
| ↳ addenda | [B31.1a](https://archive.org/details/gov.law.asme.b31.1a.2008), [B31.1b](https://archive.org/details/gov.law.asme.b31.1b.2009) | 2008, 2009 | Changes to B31.1-2007 | |
| Liquid pipelines | [ASME B31.4 Pipeline Transportation Systems for Liquid Hydrocarbons and Other Liquids](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/cfr/ibr/002/asme.b31.4.2002.pdf) | 2002 | Crude, condensate, natural gasoline, NGL, LPG, CO₂, liquid alcohol, anhydrous ammonia and products, from lease facilities to terminals, with pump stations | para. 400.1 |
| Refrigeration | [ASME B31.5 Refrigeration Piping and Heat Transfer Components](https://archive.org/details/gov.law.asme.b31.5.2001) | 2001 | Refrigerant and secondary coolant piping | para. 500.1 |

| Gas pipeline integrity | [ASME B31.8S Managing System Integrity of Gas Pipelines](https://law.resource.org/pub/us/cfr/ibr/002/asme.b31.8s.2004.pdf) | 2004 | Integrity management programs for onshore gas pipelines | |

Open the addenda alongside their base PDF, because they amend it.

## Scopes no free PDF covers

No free PDF covers these scopes, and none of the ten PDFs can stand in for them, so you would need to buy the code.

| Scope | Code to buy | Current edition and list price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Process plants: refineries, chemical, pharmaceutical, semiconductor, cryogenic and gas processing plants | [B31.3 Process Piping](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b31-3-process-piping) | 2024, $695 (PDF) |
| Hydrogen piping and pipelines | [B31.12 Hydrogen Piping and Pipelines](https://www.asme.org/Codes-Standards/Find-Codes-Standards/B31-12-Hydrogen-Piping-Pipelines) | 2023, $335 |
| Slurry pipelines | [B31.4 Liquids and Slurries](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b31-4-pipeline-transportation-systems-liquids-slurries) (slurries were in the separate B31.11 when B31.4-2002 was issued) | 2025, $325 |
| Pipeline personnel qualification | [B31Q Pipeline Personnel Qualification](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b31q-pipeline-personnel-qualification) | 2025, $385 |

ASME is moving the hydrogen pipeline rules from B31.12 into a new chapter of B31.8, according to an [October 2024 ASME release](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/media-inquiries/press-releases/prci-provides-guidance-for-the-addition-of-hydrogen-pipeline-requirements-to-asme-b31-8-gas-transmission-and-distribution-piping-systems-standard).
