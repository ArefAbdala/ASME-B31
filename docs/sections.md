# The PDF library: which PDF covers which scope

Eight free PDFs cover six of the seven B31 construction scopes; only hydrogen (B31.12) has none. They are editions from 2004 to 2020 that US regulations incorporate by reference, published by Public.Resource.Org. This site hosts the four that fit under GitHub's 100 MB file limit; the four larger ones open on the Internet Archive.

These editions are good for learning the Code and finding the right section. For design or compliance work, use the edition your regulator or contract names, usually a newer one (see [sources](sources.md)).

| Scope | Open this PDF | Edition | Covers | Start at |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power and central heating | [ASME B31.1 Power Piping](https://archive.org/download/pub.cfr.asme.b31.01.2016/asme.b31.01.2016.pdf) (Internet Archive, 326 MB) | 2016 | Power stations, industrial and institutional plants, geothermal and district heating, boiler external piping | para. 100.1 |
| Process plants | [ASME B31.3 Process Piping](https://archive.org/download/pub.cfr.asme.b31.03.2020/asme.b31.03.2020.pdf) (Internet Archive, 437 MB) | 2020 | Refineries, chemical, pharmaceutical, semiconductor, cryogenic and gas processing plants | para. 300 |
| Liquid and slurry pipelines | [ASME B31.4 Pipeline Transportation Systems for Liquids and Slurries](https://archive.org/download/pub.cfr.asme.b31.04.2016/asme.b31.04.2016.pdf) (Internet Archive, 158 MB) | 2016 | Crude, condensate, NGL, LPG, CO₂, liquid alcohol, anhydrous ammonia, products, water, brine, biofuels and slurries, with pump stations and terminals | para. 400.1 |
| Refrigeration | [ASME B31.5 Refrigeration Piping and Heat Transfer Components](pdfs/asme.b31.05.2016.pdf) | 2016 | Refrigerant, heat transfer component and secondary coolant piping | para. 500.1 |
| Gas pipelines and distribution | [ASME B31.8 Gas Transmission and Distribution Piping Systems](https://archive.org/download/pub.cfr.asme.b31.08.2016/asme.b31.08.2016.pdf) (Internet Archive, 246 MB) | 2016 | Transmission, gathering, compressor and regulating stations, mains and service lines | para. 802 |
| Gas pipeline integrity | [ASME B31.8S Managing System Integrity of Gas Pipelines](pdfs/asme.b31.8s.2004.pdf) | 2004 | Integrity management programs for onshore gas pipelines | |
| Corroded pipelines | [ASME B31G Manual for Determining the Remaining Strength of Corroded Pipelines](pdfs/asme.b31g.2012.pdf) | 2012 | Remaining strength of corroded pipe in B31.4, B31.8, B31.11 and B31.12 pipelines | |
| Buildings | [ASME B31.9 Building Services Piping](pdfs/asme.b31.09.2014.pdf) | 2014 | Piping in industrial, institutional, commercial and public buildings and multi-unit residences | para. 900.1 |

The larger PDFs take a while to load. Download them once if you will use them often.

## Scopes no free PDF covers

No free PDF covers these, and none of the free PDFs can stand in for them, so you would need to buy the code. Current editions of the sections above are listed here too where they matter most for design work.

| Scope | Code to buy | Current edition and list price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Process plants, current edition (the free 2020 edition is above) | [B31.3 Process Piping](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b31-3-process-piping) | 2024, $695 (PDF) |
| Hydrogen piping and pipelines | [B31.12 Hydrogen Piping and Pipelines](https://www.asme.org/Codes-Standards/Find-Codes-Standards/B31-12-Hydrogen-Piping-Pipelines) | 2023, $335 |
| Pipeline personnel qualification | [B31Q Pipeline Personnel Qualification](https://www.asme.org/codes-standards/find-codes-standards/b31q-pipeline-personnel-qualification) | 2025, $385 |

ASME is moving the hydrogen pipeline rules from B31.12 into a new chapter of B31.8, according to an [October 2024 ASME release](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/media-inquiries/press-releases/prci-provides-guidance-for-the-addition-of-hydrogen-pipeline-requirements-to-asme-b31-8-gas-transmission-and-distribution-piping-systems-standard).
