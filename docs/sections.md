## Notes

- **B31.3 scope is wide.** The 2024 page names hydrogen and power-generation plants as well as refineries, chemical, pharmaceutical, textile, paper and pulp, semiconductor and cryogenic plants.
- **B31.5 has hard exclusions.** It reaches down to −320°F (−196°C), but it does not apply to water piping or to piping at 15 psi or below.
- **B31.8 reaches the customer.** Besides transmission lines and stations, it covers gas mains and service lines.
- **Hydrogen is moving.** An [ASME release of October 2024](https://www.asme.org/about-asme/media-inquiries/press-releases/prci-provides-guidance-for-the-addition-of-hydrogen-pipeline-requirements-to-asme-b31-8-gas-transmission-and-distribution-piping-systems-standard) says B31.12's pipeline rules are being translated into a new hydrogen chapter of B31.8, targeted for its 2026 edition. A March 2026 [AWS Welding Journal article](https://www.aws.org/magazines-and-media/welding-journal/2026/march/an-overview-of-asme-b31-codes-and-standards/) refers to a "planned transition away from B31.12". B31.12-2023 is still the edition on sale, so use it until ASME publishes the change.

## What each section covers

Each section is written for one kind of facility, so the facility type is the first filter. Scope sentences come from the asme.org pages. The typical systems and the "watch for" column are common practice; check them in the section's own scope chapter.

| Section | Written for | Typical systems | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B31.1 Power Piping | Power stations, industrial and institutional plants, geothermal heating, central and district heating and cooling | Main steam, feedwater, boiler external piping, plant steam and condensate | Boiler external piping is governed jointly with BPVC Section I |
| B31.3 Process Piping | Refineries and chemical, pharmaceutical, hydrogen, textile, paper and pulp, power-generation, semiconductor and cryogenic plants | Hydrocarbon, chemical, utility (steam, air, water) and cryogenic piping inside the plant | Fluid service categories (Normal, D, M, High Pressure, High Purity) drive inspection and testing |
| B31.4 Liquids and Slurries | Liquid and slurry pipelines between producing leases, tank farms, plants and terminals | Crude, products, NGL, LPG, CO₂, ammonia, liquid alcohol and aqueous slurry lines, with pump and metering stations | Piping inside a refinery or process plant is B31.3, not B31.4 |
| B31.5 Refrigeration | Refrigerant, heat transfer component and secondary coolant piping, down to −320°F (−196°C) | Packaged and field-erected refrigeration systems, brine and glycol loops | Excludes water piping and piping at 15 psi or below |
| B31.8 Gas Transmission and Distribution | Gas pipelines, compressor stations, metering and regulating stations, gas mains and service lines | Transmission lines, distribution mains, gathering lines, service lines up to the meter | Location class (population density) sets the design factor |
| B31.9 Building Services | Industrial, institutional, commercial and public buildings, and multi-unit residences, within the property limits | Heating hot water, chilled water, low-pressure steam and condensate, compressed air in buildings | Has size, pressure and temperature limits; above them, use B31.1 |
| B31.12 Hydrogen | Gaseous hydrogen and hydrogen mixtures in piping and pipelines, and liquid hydrogen piping | Hydrogen plant piping (Part IP) and hydrogen pipelines (Part PL) | Hydrogen pipeline rules are moving into B31.8 (see above) |
