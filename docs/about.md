# About this site

This site is the published form of the [ArefAbdala/ASME-B31](https://github.com/ArefAbdala/ASME-B31) repository. The pages under **Find your section** are the repository's guide documents; the pages under **Study** and **Practice** add reading maps, explanations and self-checks on top of them.

## Ground rules

- **Only legitimate sources.** Every PDF link goes to the copy that Public.Resource.Org posts on law.resource.org or the Internet Archive, because US regulations incorporate those editions by reference. Current editions link only to ASME's official pages. No copies of the standards are stored in the repository or on this site.
- **No Code text.** The study pages explain ideas in their own words and cite paragraph numbers so you can read the rule itself in the PDF. Short numerical facts (a test factor, a design factor) are quoted with their paragraph so you can check them.
- **Older editions.** The free PDFs date from 1991 to 2009. Paragraph numbers and values on the study pages are from those editions and may differ in current ones.

## Page links

Reading-map links open the PDF at a specific page, written as **PDF p.** (the page number your PDF viewer shows). The printed page number at the foot of each Code page is lower, because the PDFs start with a cover sheet and front matter. Most browsers' built-in PDF viewers honor the page jump; if yours does not, use the viewer's page box.
