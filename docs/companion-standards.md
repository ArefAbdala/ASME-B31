## Withdrawn sections

- **B31.11 (Slurry Transportation Piping Systems)** was folded into B31.4, which is why B31.4's title reads "Liquids and Slurries". B31E-2008 still lists B31.11 because it predates the merger.
- **B31.2 (Fuel Gas Piping)** is withdrawn. Fuel gas piping in buildings follows NFPA 54 / ANSI Z223.1, the National Fuel Gas Code.

ASME's own withdrawal notices could not be reached, so these two points come from general knowledge rather than an ASME page.
