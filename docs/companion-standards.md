# Companion standards and withdrawn sections

Two companion standards have free PDFs, and both support gas and liquid pipelines.

| Standard | Edition | Use with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ASME B31.8S Managing System Integrity of Gas Pipelines](pdfs/asme.b31.8s.2004.pdf) | 2004 | B31.8: integrity management programs for onshore gas pipelines |
| [ASME B31G Manual for Determining the Remaining Strength of Corroded Pipelines](pdfs/asme.b31g.2012.pdf) | 2012 | B31.4, B31.8, B31.11 and B31.12 Part PL pipelines: remaining strength of corroded pipe |

Other companion standards (B31J, B31P, B31T, B31E) have no free PDF. For study, each PDF in the library carries its own rules on flexibility, heat treatment and toughness, so you do not need them to follow the base Code.

## Withdrawn sections

- **B31.11 (Slurry Transportation Piping Systems)** was a separate section. It was folded into B31.4, which is why B31.4 is titled "Liquids and Slurries"; the free B31.4-2016 covers slurry pipelines in Chapter XI.
- **B31.2 (Fuel Gas Piping)** is withdrawn. The B31.1-2016 Introduction sends fuel gas piping from the point of delivery to each appliance to ANSI Z223.1, the National Fuel Gas Code (NFPA 54).
